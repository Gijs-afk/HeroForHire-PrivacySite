Hero For Hire

An idle guild game for phones.

Support Privacy policy

You run a guild in a quiet town. Summon heroes, send them on quests and build up the town around them, a little at a time. Your heroes keep working while the game is closed.

Questions

My hero went missing. Can I get him back?

When a party loses and one hero does not come back, he is Missing. A quest called Rescue with his name then stands at the top of the quest board.

  • You have 48 hours from the moment the party came home. The clock runs while the game is closed.
  • Send other heroes on it. It is the same fight as before, and you get one try.
  • If the rescue wins, he comes home and needs time to recover. If it fails, or nobody is sent in time, he is gone.
  • An Escape Scroll in storage saves a hero who would fall, so there is no rescue at all. The Library writes them.

With notifications on, the game reminds you 12 hours before a rescue runs out.

Where is my save? What happens if I uninstall?

Your guild is saved only on your phone. There is no account and no login, and the game has no cloud save yet.

Uninstalling the game deletes the save from your phone. If your phone makes backups (Google backup or iCloud), a copy may be kept in that backup; your phone's backup settings decide that. A guild does not move to a new phone by itself.

How do ads work? How do I change my privacy choice?

An ad only plays when you tap a button with the ad mark, for example to cut 30 minutes off a hero's rest or a building upgrade, or to get quests on an empty board. There are no ads you did not ask for, and only a few can be watched each day. If an ad is closed early, nothing is given.

All ads in the game are non-personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before the first ad. To change that choice later, open Settings and tap Privacy choices, under Other. That button only shows where the law asks for it.

More in the privacy policy.

How do notifications work? How do I turn them off?

The game can tell you when your heroes are home, when an enemy waits for your answer (fight or flee) and when a rescue is about to run out. It sends at most two a day and none between 22:00 and 08:00.

Turn them off in Settings, under Notifications: one switch for all, or one for each kind. You can also turn them off in your phone's settings. They are planned on your phone; no server sends them.

What is the Guild Charter?

The Guild Charter is a planned one-time purchase. When it is available, it gives the time rewards of ads without watching the ad, inside the same daily limit, and a banner in the guild hall. It is bought once, not monthly, and can be restored on a new phone with Restore purchases.

Is anything random sold?

No. Nothing you can buy, with money or with gems, gives a random result. Summons, crystals, items and gold are never sold. Gems are only earned in play. The Escape Scroll and anything to do with a rescue are never for sale.
