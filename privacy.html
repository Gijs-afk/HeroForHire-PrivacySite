Privacy policy

Hero For Hire, the mobile game

Support Privacy policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

In short

  • There is no account and no login. Your game is saved only on your phone.
  • We, the developer, do not receive your game data.
  • The game shows ads only when you ask for one. All ads are non-personalised. Google, who serves the ads, receives some data from your phone to do that.
  • When purchases are available, RevenueCat helps us check what you bought.
  • Notifications are planned on your phone. No server sends them.
  • The game has no analytics and no crash reporting.

Who we are

Hero For Hire is made by Gies Games, a developer in the Netherlands. In this policy, "we" means Gies Games. We are the controller for the personal data described here, as meant in the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Contact: gieske166@gmail.com

Your game and your save

Your guild, heroes, items, gems and settings are stored in a file on your phone. The game does not send this file to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, change it or restore it.

Your phone may include the file in its own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone). That backup is run by your phone's maker under their terms, not by us.

Ads

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads. An ad only plays after you tap a button with the ad mark. We never show an ad you did not ask for.

What Google receives

When the game asks for an ad, and while an ad plays, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game sends data to Google. According to Google, this is:

  • your IP address, which can show your rough location (such as your city or region);
  • what happens with the ad: app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic data, such as how the app performs;
  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android or the app set ID.

Google uses this data to show ads, to measure them, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to prevent fraud. The data is encrypted while it is sent.

Non-personalised ads only

The game asks Google to show only non-personalised ads, for every player in every country. These ads are not based on a profile of you. They are chosen by context, such as the app and your rough location. Google may still use cookies or device identifiers for the purposes above.

On iPhone, the game does not ask for permission to track you, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Ads in the game are limited to a content rating of PG.

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game uses Google's consent form before it asks for any ad. The form shows the first time you tap an ad button. It lists Google and its ad partners and what each does with data. You can agree, refuse, or choose per purpose. If you refuse, you can still play the whole game; you may see fewer ads or none.

Your answer is stored on your phone. To change it, open Settings in the game and tap Privacy choices, under Other.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's own policy

Google decides itself how it uses this data, as a separate controller. Read How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

When purchases are available in the game, they go through the App Store or Google Play. The store handles your payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

To check what you bought, and to let you restore it on a new phone, the game uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat then receives:

  • your purchase history in this game: which product, the price, the date and the store's receipt;
  • a random ID that the game makes for this install. It is not linked to your name or email;
  • technical data sent with each request, such as the app version, the type of phone and system, your country and currency, and your IP address.

RevenueCat processes this data for us and on our instructions. We can see purchase records in RevenueCat, but not who you are. The game also keeps a list of your purchases in its save on your phone.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game sets reminders on your phone, for example when your heroes are home. They are planned and shown by your phone. No server sends them, and no data about them leaves your phone. Turn them off in the game's Settings, under Notifications, or in your phone's settings.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you. We keep it as long as we need to help you, and no longer than two years after your last message.

What we do not do

  • No account, no login, no name or email asked in the game.
  • No analytics and no crash reporting.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No selling of personal data.

Why we may use data (legal bases)

  • Ads, in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: your consent, given in Google's consent form. Some uses for non-personalised ads, such as fraud prevention, limiting repeats and measuring ads, may rely on legitimate interests; the consent form shows which, and lets you object.
  • Ads, elsewhere: our legitimate interest in paying for the game with ads that you choose to watch.
  • Purchases: to give you what you bought (performance of a contract).
  • Support email: our legitimate interest in answering you.

How long data is kept

  • Your save: on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Your consent answer: on your phone until you change it, uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Ad data: kept by Google under its own retention rules, described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase records: kept in RevenueCat while the game offers purchases, so you can restore them, and as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Support email: no longer than two years after your last message.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, because the game has no accounts.

  • To delete your game, uninstall it. That removes the save and your consent answer from your phone. Check your phone's backup settings if you also want any copy in a backup removed.
  • To withdraw ad consent, use Privacy choices in the game's Settings, or uninstall the game.
  • To delete ad data Google holds, use your Google account controls or contact Google, and reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • To delete purchase records, email us. Tell us the store's order number, so we can find the record. Note that a deleted purchase can no longer be restored.

Data outside Europe

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area. They protect such transfers with the safeguards the GDPR allows, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, for Google, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to limit or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what was done before.

We hold very little about you ourselves. For ad data, Google is the right party to ask, and we will help where we can. Write to gieske166@gmail.com; we answer within one month.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority). You can also go to the authority in the country where you live.

Children

Hero For Hire is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. In some countries the age to give consent yourself is higher, for example 16 in the Netherlands. If you are younger than that, ask a parent or guardian before you agree in the consent form.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, for example by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The game does not send your save anywhere. Google and RevenueCat send their data encrypted. We keep the little we hold, such as support email, in accounts protected with strong passwords and two-step sign-in.

This website

This website uses no cookies, scripts or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data, such as your IP address, to run and protect its service; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game changes how it uses data, for example when purchases, analytics, crash reporting or cloud save arrive. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change needs your consent, the game will ask for it before it takes effect.
